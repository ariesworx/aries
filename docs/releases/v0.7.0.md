## New

- **`aries outcome`, experimental and CLI only** (aries-4x4vo7cq, PR 277,
  reapplied after v0.6.0). For an issue that reached an outcome, it
  gathers the per-run outcome record ([outcome-record.md](../design/outcome-record.md))
  from Aries and the CI host through `gh`, and posts it once as an
  `outcome:` comment; `--dry-run` prints it, `--backfill --since` covers
  the past. The learning loop's primary signal (epic aries-dmqqolg4).
- **`aries dispatch run|status|stop`, experimental and CLI only**
  (aries-emlsyvmv, PR 278, reapplied after v0.6.0; decision 96). The
  client-side dispatcher's loop ([dispatcher.md](../design/dispatcher.md)),
  on macOS and Linux, with `--dry-run`: it picks ready issues by their
  model label, claims each before launch, starts headless runs within the
  `dispatch/runs` lock and the budget cap, and stops on `stop`, the
  `dispatch/pause` lock, two CI failures or the cap. Its after-merge sweep
  is not in it (aries-i6fndp4h).
- **Prices by prompt length** (aries-itlyj3yw; protocol 10). A price may
  carry a tier: `aries admin price set … --over TOKENS --over-input USD …`
  gives the rates for a request whose prompt (input, cache writes and
  cache reads) is over that length, as Claude Haiku 5.5 is billed. Each
  request record is priced on its own; records that sum a turn or session
  are priced at the base rates. Migration 3 adds the tier to `prices`.
  `ariesd` of protocol 10 serves clients of 9 to 10: `ProtoMin` moves to 9, which ends v0.5.0's
  clients and the key proof they sign (`refuse_key_proof_v1`).
  Not priced: `inference_geo`'s 1.1x.
